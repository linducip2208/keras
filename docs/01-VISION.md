# 01 — Vision & Strategy

## 1. Vision Statement

> **"Sistem HRIS Indonesia yang **owner pilih sendiri** integrasinya, **karyawan pakai dari WhatsApp**, dan **HRD selesai gajian dalam 5 menit**."**

Kita bukan competitor yang ke-8. Kita melawan asumsi industri:
- Talenta mahal + lock-in. **Kita open & affordable.**
- Gadjian payroll-only. **Kita full lifecycle.**
- LinovHR enterprise-heavy. **Kita ringan untuk UKM tapi scalable.**
- KantorKu tidak punya WA bot. **Kita ESS dari WhatsApp.**
- Semua competitor punya pricing pricing/lock-in vendor. **Kita BYOK (Bring Your Own Keys) + dual SaaS + self-host.**

## 2. Mission

Menyederhanakan pengelolaan SDM bagi perusahaan Indonesia dari UKM hingga enterprise dengan:
1. **Otomasi end-to-end** — dari rekrut hingga resign, semua dalam 1 sistem
2. **Compliance Indonesia first-class** — PPh 21 TER, BPJS, UU CK, UU PDP, Kepmenaker 102 — selalu update
3. **Karyawan-first UX** — bisa diakses via WhatsApp untuk blue collar/field worker
4. **Owner-friendly economics** — pricing transparan, BYOK provider, self-host option
5. **Data-driven decisions** — AI insight + predictive analytics built-in (bukan modul mahal terpisah)

## 4. Value Proposition

### Untuk Owner
- **30% cost lebih murah** dari Talenta tier setara
- **BYOK** — owner pilih sendiri provider AI/SMS/bank, ga kunci vendor
- **License-once option** — bayar sekali, pakai selamanya (untuk yg ga mau SaaS)
- **Compliance auto-update** — PPh & BPJS update tiap aturan baru, gratis

### Untuk HRD
- **Payroll 1 klik** — semua input attendance + reimburse + loan otomatis
- **Approval dari mobile/WA** — ga perlu buka laptop
- **AI screening CV** — 50 lamaran → top 10 dalam 2 menit
- **Letter generator** — SK/paklaring/SP keluar PDF dalam 30 detik

### Untuk Karyawan
- **WhatsApp ESS** — clock-in, cuti, slip gaji dari WA (no app install)
- **Mobile app native** (Flutter iOS+Android) untuk yang lebih heavy
- **Slip gaji dengan password** (NIK/DOB) — aman dibagi via WA
- **Cuti ulang tahun** — perk yg ga ada di competitor

### Untuk C-Level
- **AI narrative insight** — "turnover dept A naik 15% karena…"
- **Predictive turnover risk** — flag karyawan high-risk resign sebelum kejadian
- **Compensation benchmark** vs pasar (data partner)
- **Custom report builder** drag-drop + scheduled email

## 6. Business Model

### Revenue Streams

**1. SaaS Subscription** (primary, recurring)
- 4 tier: Starter, Basic, Essential, Suite
- Billed monthly atau annual (discount 15% annual)
- Per-user pricing

**2. License-once + Annual Maintenance** (one-time + recurring)
- License perpetual senilai 18 bulan SaaS
- Annual maintenance 20% dari license value
- Self-host atau private cloud (kita deploy)
- Includes 1 tahun update gratis, beyond itu maintenance

**3. Implementation & Migration Service** (one-time)
- Free tier: starter (self-onboard)
- Paid tier: dedicated CS + data migration + training (Rp 5–25 juta tergantung scope)

**4. Whitelabel Reseller** (recurring)
- Partner reseller daerah/vertical
- 30% lifetime revenue share
- Custom branding via license v3

**5. Marketplace Add-ons** (transactional, future)
- Background check (per check)
- Bank validation API (per validation)
- E-sign quota (per signature)
- LLM AI usage (cost-plus, owner bawa key sendiri optional)

### Cost Structure

| Item | % of revenue |
|---|---|
| Infrastructure (server, DB, storage) | 8% |
| Customer success & support | 15% |
| Engineering | 25% |
| Sales & marketing | 22% |
| Compliance & legal | 5% |
| Operations & ops | 5% |
| **Gross margin target** | **75%+** |

### Pricing Strategy
- **Anchor:** Talenta Rp 25–50k/user → kita Rp 15–25k = 40–50% saving message
- **Differentiator:** BYOK + license-once option
- **Loss leader:** Starter Rp 10k untuk akuisisi awal, upsell ke Basic via fitur lock (recruitment, OKR, LMS)

### Differentiation Statement

### Battle Cards (vs competitor utama)

**vs Mekari Talenta**
- ✅ Kita: 40% lebih murah, BYOK, WA bot, license-once
- ⚠️ Talenta: lebih mature, brand kuat, ekosistem Mekari (Jurnal, KlikPajak)
- 💡 Win condition: target UKM yang merasa Talenta terlalu mahal & lock-in

**vs Gadjian**
- ✅ Kita: full lifecycle (recruitment, OKR, LMS), modern UX
- ⚠️ Gadjian: payroll lebih dewasa, brand UKM kuat
- 💡 Win condition: tunjukkan ROI multi-modul vs payroll-only

**vs KantorKu**
- ✅ Kita: WA bot, license-once, no-hardcoded providers, AI insight
- ⚠️ KantorKu: ISO 27001 cert, dukungan account manager
- 💡 Win condition: BYOK + ekosistem dynamic

**vs LinovHR**
- ✅ Kita: SaaS-friendly, ringan, harga jelas
- ⚠️ LinovHR: succession & talent management lebih dalam
- 💡 Win condition: target SMB yang LinovHR over-spec

## 8. Why Now (Market Timing)

1. **UU PDP berlaku efektif Oktober 2024** — perusahaan butuh sistem yang compliant
2. **PPh 21 TER 2024** — semua HRIS harus update; momentum switching
3. **Coretax DJP launching 2026** — integration opportunity
4. **AI mainstream** — owner sudah familiar dengan ChatGPT; CV screening AI tidak lagi sci-fi
5. **WhatsApp Business API matang** — bot ESS feasible secara cost
6. **Talenta IPO Mekari 2024-2025** — harga naik, ada appetite cari alternatif
7. **UKM digitalization pasca-pandemi** — willingness to pay untuk efisiensi naik

## 9. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Talenta agresif lower pricing | High | Differentiate via BYOK + WA bot — bukan price war |
| Compliance perubahan PPh/BPJS mendadak | High | Auto-update engine + tim compliance dedicated |
| Customer enterprise butuh on-premise | Medium | License-once self-host model dari Day 1 |
| Server downtime payroll-day (tanggal 1) | High | Multi-region failover, payroll batch async queue |
| Vendor WA Business API ban | Medium | Multi-vendor adapter (per CLAUDE.md no-hardcoded) |
| Akuisisi karyawan lambat (chicken-egg) | Medium | Free Starter tier + referral program |
| Data breach payroll/PII | Critical | Encryption at rest, 2FA mandatory, audit log, pen-test annual |

## 10. North Star Metric

**Weekly Active Payroll Companies (WAPC)** — jumlah perusahaan yang menjalankan minimal 1 transaksi payroll/attendance/approval per minggu.

Alasan: ini paling reflect engagement nyata. Karyawan login lihat slip = passive. Tapi HRD/owner yang touch sistem = product is sticky.

Target Year 1: 1.500 WAPC.
